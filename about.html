Who We Are

About GEPAM Science Hub

GEPAM Science Hub is an educational platform created to support students learning Physics and Chemistry from Form 1 to Form 6. Our goal is to make science learning easier by bringing useful learning resources together in one accessible online platform.

Our Purpose

Making Science Easier to Learn

Science becomes more manageable when students can access clear explanations, relevant examination materials and organized resources. GEPAM Science Hub is built around this idea: making important Physics and Chemistry learning resources easier to find, understand and use.

Our Mission

Simplify Science Education

To provide accessible and affordable Physics and Chemistry learning resources that help students understand science, practise effectively and build confidence in their studies.

Our Vision

A Future Where Science Feels Possible

To grow into a trusted science learning platform that helps make Physics and Chemistry easier to learn and more accessible to students across Tanzania.
