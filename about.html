ℹ️ About GEPAM Science Hub

An educational platform dedicated to making Physics and Chemistry simple.

🎯 Vision & Mission

GEPAM Science Hub is an educational platform dedicated to making Physics and Chemistry learning simple, accessible and effective for students from Form 1 to Form 6.

Our Mission: To simplify science education by providing quality, affordable and accessible learning materials that help students build confidence and achieve better results.

Our Vision: To become a leading science learning platform in Tanzania by making Physics and Chemistry easy for every student.

📚 What We Offer

  • 📖 Science Notes: Simple Physics and Chemistry notes from Form 1 to Form 6 tailored to your syllabus.
  • 📝 Past Papers: Examinations from Annual, Mock, Joint, Pre-NECTA and NECTA organized with a modern folder layout.
  • 🎯 Examination Preparation: Premium resources designed to improve understanding, confidence and academic performance.

⭐ Why Choose GEPAM Science Hub?

✔ Simple explanations for complex scientific theories.

✔ NECTA focused high-quality learning materials.

✔ Affordable educational resources for every student.

✔ Full learning support for Physics and Chemistry students.
